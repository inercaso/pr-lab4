PERCENTILE EXPLANATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Mean: average of all latency measurements
  - Median (p50): 50% of requests completed faster than this
  - p90: 90% of requests completed faster than this
  - p95: 95% of requests completed faster than this (tail latency)

  p90 and p95 are important for understanding worst-case behavior.
  high tail latencies indicate occasional slow responses.

WHY LATENCY INCREASES WITH QUORUM:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  with quorum-based replication, the leader waits for K followers
  to acknowledge before responding. each follower has random delay.

  - quorum=1: wait for FASTEST follower only (low latency)
  - quorum=3: wait for 3rd fastest (median latency)
  - quorum=5: wait for SLOWEST follower (high latency)

  this is the consistency vs latency trade-off:
  - higher quorum = more durable (data on more nodes)
  - higher quorum = higher latency (wait for slower nodes)

RACE CONDITION EXPLANATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  consistency = % of (key, follower) pairs where values match leader.
  race conditions from concurrent writes + network delays cause mismatches:

  - with quorum=K, K followers ACK before leader responds
  - but per-message delays cause writes to arrive out of order
  - earlier writes with longer delays can overwrite later writes
  - higher quorum = more synchronized followers = higher consistency

  theoretical = (quorum / num_followers) * 100%
  (assumes K followers are fully consistent, others have 0% match)
